<objective>
Stop the watch treadmill: a persistently-failing degraded repo is not re-built on every debounced save at an unchanged sha, while any source change (or an explicit jarvis index) re-triggers the full build. Ships the pure skip predicate, the _cmd_watch wiring, and the watch tri-state flag.

Purpose: Self-heal (retry every run) + watch (reindex on every save) would otherwise combine into a doomed multi-minute build per save cycle — the anti-treadmill rule is FALL-05 (Pitfall 5 in 03-RESEARCH.md).

Output: _watch_should_retry_full_build + _watch_skip_check helpers, _cmd_watch._reindex skip consult and fallback flag pass-through, watch_parser --fallback-search-only pair, parser/helper/wiring tests.

D-IDs implemented: Area 2 (watch accepts the same tri-state flag; watch is just another reindex driver), Area 3 (watch retry-skip keying: persist the last full-build attempt sha — carried by the degraded terminal write's commit_sha from Plan 03-01; skip only when sha unchanged AND row degraded).
</objective>
